Things only we understand

“Ice cream khaogi?”
Yes / No
“…Kashmir jaogi?” 🎶
You’re unbelievably clumsy.
Tripping. Slipping. Falling for no reason.

And somehow still landing
right into my heart every time.
You do this thing.
You gently play with my belly like it’s the most natural thing in the world.

No words. No reason.
Just… us.
You never run out of ways to say you love me.
Sometimes in words.
Sometimes randomly.
Sometimes when I least expect it.

And I hope you know —
I hear every single one.
I forgot what we argue about.
Which probably means it was never that important
in the first place.
